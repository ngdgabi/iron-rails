# Iron Rails: Blue & Gray

**Native 120Hz Metal Comic-Arcade Continental Railroad Strategy & Real-Time Action Game**  
*Universal Purchase for macOS 14+, iOS 16+, and iPadOS 16+ (`com.ngd.ironrails`)*

Inspired by the joyful 1989 Commodore 64 & Amiga strategy-action classic — built from scratch in Swift & SpriteKit with **100% original IP, procedural comic artwork, and dual-chip real-time audio synthesis**.

---

## Four Classic Modes in One Unified Game

1. **1861–1864 Continental Railway Strategy Map**: Command **Union Blue** or **Gray Corps** across 14 interconnected North American states. Protect connected rail stations so the **Paymaster Gold Express** funds fresh armies (`5 Gold = +1 Army`), hold Carolinas Port for overseas **Atlantic Clipper Ship** reinforcements, merge battalions into `3•3•3` Grand Corps, and watch out for roaming **Storm Clouds**!
2. **Real-Time River-Bridge Field Battles**: Switch live (`Tab` / `UNIT`) between **Range-Gauge Field Artillery** (blast the 3-span wooden river bridge into splinters!), **Bugle-Charging Saber Cavalry**, and **Synchronized Musket Infantry**.
3. **High-Speed Moving Steam Train Heists**: Leap across swaying bullion boxcar roofs at 120Hz, duck overhead signal gantries, and knock out guards with slapstick dizzy punches to seize the Steam Locomotive Cab!
4. **60-Second Timber Fort Assaults**: Scale 3-tier wooden ramparts, jump rolling TNT barrels & guard hounds, and raise your colors on the bastion flagpole before the dynamite fuse burns out!
5. **Instant 1989 ↔ 2026 Time-Machine**: Press **`V`** & **`B`** (or tap **`ERA`** & **`CHIP`**) anytime to morph live between **120Hz HD Comic Art + Brass/Ragtime Synth**, **CRT Arcade Cabinet**, and **16-Color 1989 C64 Pixel Art + 3-Channel MOS 6581 SID Chiptune**!

## Build & Run

```bash
# Build Universal 2 (Apple Silicon arm64 + Intel x86_64) App, DMG & ZIP:
make release

# Launch on macOS:
open "dist/Iron Rails.app"

# Verify macOS & iOS/iPadOS Xcode Release targets:
make macos
make ios
```
